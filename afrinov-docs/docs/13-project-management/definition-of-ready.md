# Definition of Ready

**Status:** DRAFT · **Owner:** Product/Engineering · **Last verified against code:** 4e6d76f, 2026-10-09

A piece of work is ready to start when: its Business Requirement and
Functional Requirement(s) exist and are approved; its place in the domain
model (`03-domain/`) is defined; any open assumptions it depends on
(`00-governance/assumptions-register.md`) are resolved or explicitly
accepted as risk.

*Unverified:* a team agreement; not recorded or enforced in the repository.
