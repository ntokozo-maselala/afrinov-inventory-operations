# Production Readiness — Summary

Go-live is gated on the full `readiness-checklist.md`, `uat-plan.md`
sign-off, and `acceptance-criteria.md` being met. The final decision to cut
over rests with the business owner, informed by the migration reconciliation
report (`07-data/migration-strategy.md`) — not by engineering alone, since
this is the moment the spreadsheets stop being authoritative.
